    Our charities

    Every rand from the 10/10 goes to two scholarship funds that put kids through school in Johannesburg. Since 2018 the challenge has raised .

    Ruth First Jeppe Memorial Trust

    Full tuition scholarships for girls at Jeppe High School for Girls, aiming to develop them into “principled leaders who are making a difference in the world”. Named after Ruth First, a Jeppe Old Girl remembered for her fight against injustice. Formed in 2010; its patron is Justice Albie Sachs.

    • 68 girls on scholarships now
    • 14 joined in 2026
    • 120 alumnae

    Figures from the trust’s website, November 2026.

    Visit the Ruth First Jeppe Trust

    Theo Jackson Scholarship Fund

    Scholarships for boys at Jeppe High School for Boys who come from financially disadvantaged, orphaned or single-parent homes. It pays tuition and boarding, and covers uniforms, sports kit, tours and stationery when families can’t. Founded in 2005 by Dale and Craig Jackson in memory of their father Theo, an orphan who found a family at Jeppe and co-founded the Jeppe Quondam Athletic Club.

    • Up to 10 new scholars a year
    • Grew from 1 boy in 2006 to 42 by 2012

    Figures from the fund’s page on the Jeppe Boys website.

    Visit the Theo Jackson Fund
